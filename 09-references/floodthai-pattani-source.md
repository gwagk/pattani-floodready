# FloodThai Pattani — Forecast Reference Source
Registered: 2026-10-08 (Asia/Bangkok)
URL: https://www.floodthai.org/pattani
Purpose: secondary dashboard for Pattani FloodReady forecasts and situational awareness.

## What it provides
- 24-hour rain forecast (attribution on site: Open-Meteo)
- River/canal station water levels and trends (site attributes station data to Thaiwater/หน่วยงานต้นทาง)
- Maps, radar links, community water reports, CCTV and emergency numbers
- Site currently states Pattani provincial command center is not activated on this platform; this is **not** a declaration that official government incident command is inactive.

## Operational use and validation
1. Use FloodThai for early screening and cross-checking, not as the sole authoritative warning source.
2. For forecast, compare TMD warnings and forecasts: https://www.tmd.go.th/
3. For observations, verify station ID, observation time, water level and trend with original providers (Thaiwater, RID, DWR).
4. Record issue time and retrieval time; do not conflate forecast rainfall with observed rainfall.
5. Include upstream rainfall, 24/72-hour accumulated rainfall, river stage and road access in decisions.
6. No flood/no-flood categorical prediction from a single map or dashboard.
7. If evacuation order or unsafe exit routes are reported, prioritize immediate safety over further forecasting.
